How It Works

1

Open any PDF Report

Navigate to Layout Editor → PDF Reports, pick the Invoice report and click Layout. (Quotations, Orders & Delivery Slips are available with PRO.)

2

Pick a starting template

Start from the built-in Classic template, or apply one of your own saved templates with a single click. (Modern, Compact & Letterhead with PRO.)

3

Design your layout

Drag blocks from the sidebar onto the canvas. Configure each block’s properties in the right panel. Adjust page margins, column order, fonts, alignment — everything visual, no coding.

4

Activate & reuse

Toggle the layout to Active and it automatically replaces the default report output. Save your design as a template to reuse it on other documents, or share it across your organization.
